Four seats, one pot

Everyone antes the same stake. Finish closest to 21 without going over and the whole pot is yours.

  1. Board, Hand, or Lock. On your turn, call the next shared Board card, draw a private Hand card (two per hand), or Lock your total.
  2. Ride or Lock. When anyone calls the Board, each open seat decides blind: ride the card, or lock before it lands.
  3. Raise or fold. Once per board, a seat can raise half the ante. Call to stay in. Highest total at or under 21 wins; ties split.
