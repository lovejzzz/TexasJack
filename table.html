Three rivals. One clean read.

Four seats. One shared fuse.

Everyone starts with a private card. Board cards help every seat that has not locked yet. Get close to 21, read three distinct rivals, and win the whole pot.

  1. Ante. All four seats pay the same stake into one pot.
  2. Board. Draw one shared card. It counts for every active seat and can break several players at once.
  3. Hand. Take a private card that only helps or hurts you. You get two per hand.
  4. Lock. Freeze your current number. Future board cards cannot change it, but you still have to answer later bets.
  5. Pressure. Once per board state, any seat can raise half the ante. Call to stay in or fold the whole hand.
  6. Showdown. Highest total at or below 21 takes the pot. Ties split it.
