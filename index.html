How to play

Blackjack with a shared board. Get closer to 21 than the dealer — but every card in the middle counts for both of you.

  1. Read the board. You and the dealer each hold one private card. The dealer's is face down, but the board pins them to a range.
  2. Board or Hand. Send each card to the board, where it lands in the dealer's hand too while they're drawing, or keep it in your hand — twice per hand.
  3. Stand and show down. The dealer flips and plays to beat you. Bust and you lose, even if they bust too.
  4. Climb the ladder. Each dealer plays from a purse. Break it to collect their bounty and move up; lose your stake and you drop a rung.
Press & the Counter
  • Press adds to your bet after a new card. The earlier you press, the bigger the offer.
  • Counter. Pressing makes the dealer bolder: they may draw once more to chase you. Beat it and earn a bounty of +30%, +60% or +100% of the ante, by Pressure level.
  • House rules. Mara never counters. Vex chases one level hotter than you pressed. Sol takes board cards and draws until 18.
