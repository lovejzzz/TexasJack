How to play

Blackjack with a shared board. Get closer to 21 than the dealer — but every card in the middle counts for both of you.

  1. Read the board. You and the dealer each hold one private card. The dealer's is face down, but the board pins them to a range.
  2. Bet. Both of you ante. Before the draw and after each card you take there is a betting round: check or bet; facing a bet, call, raise or fold. Bets are fixed: one ante, then two.
  3. Board or Hand. Send a card to the board, where it lands in the dealer's hand too while they're drawing, or keep it in your hand — twice per hand. Then stand and show down.
  4. Climb the ladder. Each dealer plays from a purse and the ante rises every two hands. Whoever can't post the ante loses the match.
The three dealers
  • Mara, The Rock never bluffs. When she raises, believe her — and your bluffs work best on her.
  • Vex, The Shark bluffs about one bet in five and may raise twice a round. Don't get pushed off a fair hand.
  • Sol, The Spark calls almost anything and draws to 18. Don't bluff him; bet your good hands.
  • Dealers never see your private cards or the next card off the shoe.
