How to play Texas Jack

Blackjack's race to 21, played with Hold'em's shared board. The twist: the card you ask for might end up helping the dealer too.

  1. The deal. You get one card, the dealer gets one face down, and one community card hits the middle. Community cards count toward both totals.
  2. When you hit, the dealer reacts at the same moment. If the dealer's total is under 17 they hit as well — and your card lands in the community row, feeding both hands. If the dealer stands, the card is yours alone.
  3. When you stand, the dealer draws privately until they reach 17 or bust.
  4. Double is available on your first action: double the wager, take one card, then stand automatically.
  5. Bust over 21 and you lose. If a community card busts you both, it's a push.
